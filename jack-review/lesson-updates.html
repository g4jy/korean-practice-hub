Bring reviewed lesson words into every mode

New words appear in Words, Study and Quiz, and in the standalone flashcards. Explicit “unsure” or “don’t know” observations return a word to review, even if it was marked known earlier.

Automatic delivery is not connected. Open the reviewed lesson JSON file on the device used for study. The file and learner progress stay in this browser; nothing is uploaded. Later study answers and response history are preserved.
